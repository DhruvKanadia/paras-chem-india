Our Mission

To empower Indian manufacturing industries by providing seamless, reliable, and compliant access to premium specialty and commodity chemicals from world-class global principals. We aim to be the most trusted link in the chemical supply chain, optimizing procurement for our clients.

Our Vision

To establish the benchmark for operational excellence in chemical distribution across the Indian subcontinent. We envision a future where industrial procurement is defined by transparency, technical expertise, and an unwavering commitment to sustainable practices and quality assurance.

Corporate Timeline

A history of strategic growth and expanded capabilities.

1995

Foundation

Paras Chem established its headquarters in Mumbai, initially focusing on the distribution of essential solvents for the local pharmaceutical industry, laying the groundwork for a compliance-first approach.

2005

Infrastructure Expansion

Significant investment in specialized storage facilities, enabling the safe handling and distribution of highly reactive specialty chemicals. Inauguration of our first state-of-the-art warehousing hub.

2018

Pan-India Network

Achieved comprehensive national coverage with regional hubs established across North, South, and East India, drastically reducing lead times for key industrial clients and solidifying our logistics network.
